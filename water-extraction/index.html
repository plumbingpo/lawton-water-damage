Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Across Lawton, Lawton Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Why Water Extraction Is Not a DIY Job

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. This speed difference is not just about convenience, it is about preventing thousands of dollars in additional damage that occurs with every hour water stays in contact with building materials.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.

When to Call for Water Extraction in Lawton

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A clearly labeled main water valve, in a spot everyone knows, saves time in an emergency
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working
  • Pour water into the sump pit a few times a year to confirm the pump kicks on
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
  • Program (925) 441-7917 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
